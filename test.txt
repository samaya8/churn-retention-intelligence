====================================================================
CHURN PREDICTOR — TEST SAMPLES
For use with app.py and app2.py (Streamlit apps)
====================================================================
How to use:
- Type each field into the sidebar / form of the app.
- "Actual Outcome" is the REAL historical result from Churn_Modelling.csv
  (ground truth) — compare it to what the model predicts.
- Cases 13 and 14 are made up (not real customers), built from the
  feature-importance chart to show a clearly SAFE and clearly RISKY
  profile — good for demoing the gauge chart's full range.

Verified against the saved model: 12 of these 14 cases match the
model's prediction. Cases 2 and 8 are deliberately kept even though the
model gets them wrong — XGBoost's recall is ~53% and precision ~67%
(see the notebook's Evaluation section), so it WILL miss some real
churners and flag some loyal customers. Good talking point in class.
====================================================================
